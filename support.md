# Prepwise support

Prepwise is a food tracking and meal prep app made by Chris Dunn.

## Get help
- In the app, tap **Feedback** at the top of the screen and write what happened. Your note goes straight to the
  developer, along with the app version and phone model.
- Or email <christopherdunn321@gmail.com>. A reply usually comes within a few days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Signing in with Apple is only needed for recipe research, voice and photo logging, shared grocery lists and
Friends. Everything else works without one.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open Settings in the app, then Account, then Delete my account. Apple asks you to confirm it's you. Recipes and
lists on your phone stay.

**Where does the food data come from?**
USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts.

**Why did the AI stop for today?**
Recipe research and food log reading share a daily allowance. It resets at midnight UTC, or you can add your own
key in Settings.
